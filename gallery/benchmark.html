Observable style audit, not an authorship detector

The synthetic generic paragraph contained two empty-value phrases and one vague cluster; the evidence-bound rewrite contained neither. The rule-based scan still flagged bounded phrases as possible claim-force markers, which is why its output says NOT_MEASURED_AND_NOT_INFERABLE for authorship and requires human review.

Limits

Eight synthetic cases, one generator run per condition, and two model-assisted judges are not a population estimate or human peer review. The benchmark tests evidence-packet writing; it cannot validate a real study, citation, ethics decision, or conference outcome.
