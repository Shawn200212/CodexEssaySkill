# Current scope — evidence and revision control

The current private `codex-essay-skill` release is a verification and research-record layer.
It reconciles sources, provenance, factual atoms, versions, and revision deltas for scholarly
manuscripts. Its outputs include claim–evidence–scope matrices, extraction-fidelity checks,
source and citation audits, evidence-block diagnoses, revision-control ledgers, and workflow
regression records.

It does **not** draft reader-facing prose, choose a thesis, adjudicate venue rules, copyedit, or
infer authorship. Those responsibilities belong respectively to a general or venue-specific writing
Skill, a venue overlay, Humanizer, and an explicitly requested detector diagnostic.

The historical demonstrations in this public repository remain available as an audit record. They
must not be read as a current claim of end-to-end writing performance.
