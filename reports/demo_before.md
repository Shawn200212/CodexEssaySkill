# Synthetic revision demonstration: before

> **Synthetic fixture. These are invented demonstration data, not a report of a real study.**

## Evidence packet supplied to the writer

- Six three-person teams completed one static and one adaptive 30-minute collaborative-VR session;
  order was counterbalanced.
- An AI director proposed 72 pacing interventions. A researcher approved or rejected every proposal;
  44 were enacted.
- Post-session immersion medians were 5.7/7 in the adaptive condition and 5.4/7 in the static
  condition. The packet contains no uncertainty estimate or inferential test.
- Fourteen participants described at least one intervention as restoring momentum. Seven also said
  that an intervention made the scene feel less like their own. Two participants ended a session
  early because of simulator discomfort; both sessions were excluded from the paired rating summary.
- The unit of assignment is the team/session; interview counts are participant-level.

## Draft supplied for revision

Our innovative AI-powered framework significantly empowered users and enhanced immersive experience.
The results clearly demonstrate that adaptive intelligence creates more engaging and personalized

## Why this passage fails

The paragraph invents significance, converts a small descriptive difference into causal validation,
erases researcher approval labor, substitutes participant counts for team/session units, hides
authorship resistance and early exits, and uses “empowerment,” “innovation,” and “transformation”
without naming a capability, beneficiary, cost, or boundary.
